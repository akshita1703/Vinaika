A Harmonious Symphony

Luxury &
Nature United

Vinaika Eco-Resort is Eastern India's largest and most opulent eco-sanctuary, where refined luxury intertwines seamlessly with nature's splendour. Inspired by Hindu mythology, Vinaika symbolises auspicious beginnings-mirroring the sense of renewal and tranquillity that envelops every guest.

From mud-walled cottages exuding rustic elegance to lavish suites and bespoke experiences, every detail is a masterpiece of craftsmanship. With world-class amenities, grand banquets, gourmet dining, and extraordinary service, it is the epitome of exclusivity.

What We Offer

Amenities

Luxurious Accommodations

Indulge in refined comfort with our Executive Room, Deluxe Room, Double Deluxe Room and Suite Room each thoughtfully designed with modern amenities, textured interiors, soothing lighting, and spacious washrooms to offer a serene and elegant stay.

Villa

Gulmohar Villa is a private sanctuary where elegance meets nature. Perfect for romantic escapes or peaceful retreats, it offers refined comfort, seclusion, and an experience that feels truly unforgettable.

Lawns & Banquet Halls

Celebrate in style at Rajdarbar Banquet Hall or beneath the open sky at Vrindavan, Nandanvan, and Kailash, our serene lawns designed to host truly unforgettable events.

Exquisite Dining & Bars

Savor global and regional delicacies at Nirvasa, our fine dining haven, and enjoy signature cocktails at Mahua, a cozy bar where flavours and ambiance come together to create an unforgettable dining experience.

Swimming Pool

Relax and rejuvenate at our signature pool, Vanira, a tranquil retreat set amidst lush greenery, ideal for a refreshing swim, leisurely sun lounging, or simply enjoying moments of calm in a serene setting.

Kids Play Area & Play Room

Let your kids enjoy the perfect blend of indoor fun and outdoor adventure in our vibrant kids play room and open-air play area. Featuring colorful zones like Bazighar and Natkhat, this safe, kid-friendly space encourages creativity, imagination, and active play—keeping children engaged while you relax and enjoy a worry-free stay.

Gym

Stay energized at Kaya, our fully equipped fitness center designed for wellness and movement. Whether you're into strength training or a mindful cardio session, Kaya offers a peaceful yet powerful space to stay active during your stay.

Utility Store

From travel essentials to everyday necessities, our in-house utility store, Jugaad, ensures convenience is always within reach, allowing you to enjoy a seamless and worry-free stay.

Gajraj Boardroom

Nestled in the serene surroundings of Vinaika, Gajraj is a modern, fully equipped boardroom ideal for meetings, presentations, and focused discussions. With seamless tech and a peaceful setting, it’s where ideas gain clarity and direction.

Fine Dining

A Culinary Escape
at Nirvasa

Nestled within the tranquil greens of Vinaika Eco-Resort, Nirvasa offers more than just a meal-it offers an experience. Inspired by timeless meaning and contemporary taste, Nirvasa brings together global cuisine and the local flavours of Jharkhand in a soulful, slow dining setting.

From Mediterranean classics to regional favourites, every dish is thoughtfully handcrafted and curated by an award-winning chef. Whether vegetarian or non-vegetarian, each plate tells a story, complemented by serene poolside views.

Signature Bar

A Toast to Tradition

Honoring heritage with every sip. At Mahua, every sip is a story waiting to be savored. Rooted in the heritage of Jharkhand and named after its beloved forest bloom. Mahua is where cocktails meet culture in the most soulful way.

Soft lighting, earthy textures, and leafy views create the perfect backdrop for slow conversations and spirited evenings.Whether you're toasting with friends, unwinding solo, or exploring regional infusions - Mahua is the place to be.
